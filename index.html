About

Starting in January 2027, I will pursue a PhD in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Sydney.

I graduated from Nanjing University in July 2026 with a B.Eng. in Telecommunications Engineering, ranking 1st out of 60 with a final GPA of 4.54/5.00 (90.8/100).

I was an exchange student at the National University of Singapore in the College of Design and Engineering from January to May 2025. From June to October 2025, I joined the WANDS Group at HKUST as a visiting research intern, where I worked on UAV remote identification and LoRaWAN gateway mesh systems.

I was named an Outstanding Graduate of Nanjing University and received the Joyce M. Kuok Special Award. My other honors include the Outstanding Student Award, Outstanding Student Leader Award, and Outstanding Volunteer Service Award.
